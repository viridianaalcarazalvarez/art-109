My Work

big dog big steps

This is just a small doodle of someone in my surroundings, this includes my partners pup, Ozzy. Sketching is one of my first steps of making an artwork.

pelea de gallos, 2025

This piece includes hand painted logos that you often see on the streets of Mexico that are used as advertising. This small detail is something that I truly love because of the imperfections-- they are faded, they signify that human was there.

lovely clouds

This work references a few glimpses of personal moments in my life that I wanted to share within the short span of 36 seconds. Even though this quote might not make total sense since the translation is off– it shares the real humanness of error, language, and emotion.
