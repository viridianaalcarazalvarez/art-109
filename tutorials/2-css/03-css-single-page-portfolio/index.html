pelea de gallos, 2025

This piece is an obvious showcase of a rooster fight– that may be provocative or controversial. I made this piece because it is something I have seen since I was a kid– it is big in Mexican culture but I also wanted to play homage to my father who my family would call the rooster. The piece includes hand painted logos that you often see on the streets of Mexico that are used as advertising. This small detail is something that I truly love because the way the logos are painted imperfectly, they are faded, they signify that human was there. This piece isn't really about just some roosters fighting but about my culture, my love for imperfections, and my dad of course.

lovely clouds

Lovely cloud is a video I made using hand drawn doodles that I digitalized to create a stop motion story. This work references a few glimpses of personal moments in my life that I wanted to share within the short span of 36 seconds. It includes text that comes from a random quote I found on a cheap iPhone case that is lost in translation. Even though this quote might not make total sense since the translation is off– it shares the real humanness of error, language, and emotion.
